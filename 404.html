05 — GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions.

A few things you might like to know before we begin.

01Do you visit the site?

In Ahmedabad, yes. Elsewhere, we begin with a video call and the measurements you provide.

02Who signs the structure drawings?

A qualified partner structural engineer checks and signs the structure drawings.

03Can you get municipal approval?

We prepare the drawings. Submission is handled through a licensed professional; we can connect you.

04How long does it take?

Plans take 5–7 days. A complete drawing set takes 2–3 weeks.

Yes. Tell us you'd like Vastu considerations when you get in touch.
